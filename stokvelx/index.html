FAQs

Got questions?

What is StokvelX?
+
StokvelX is a digital stokvel platform built for South African university students. It modernises the traditional rotating savings club (stokvel) concept with transparency, automation, and mobile-first design.
Is my money safe?
+
Yes. All funds are held in a dedicated escrow account. Payouts are only distributed according to the verified payout schedule. We use bank-level encryption for all transactions.
How do I receive my payout?
+
When your payout date arrives, the full pot is automatically transferred to your StokvelX wallet. You can then withdraw to your bank account via Instant EFT — usually within minutes.
What happens if someone doesn't pay?
+
Non-paying members are locked out of the platform until payment is made. Our admin team follows up and if needed, the circle admin can remove and replace defaulting members.
Can I be in multiple circles at once?
+
Yes! You can join multiple circles simultaneously. Your dashboard clearly tracks each circle's contribution schedule, payout date and balance separately.
Do I need a bank account?
+
You need a bank account for final withdrawals, but contributions can be made via EFT, card or mobile wallet. Your StokvelX wallet holds funds securely in the meantime.

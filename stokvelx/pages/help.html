1. Getting Started

▼

Welcome to StokvelX — South Africa's first student savings circle platform. Follow these steps to get your account up and running.

1

Create your account

Go to the Sign Up page and complete all 3 steps: enter your personal details (name, email, phone), your student information (university, student number, ID number), and create a secure password. Your ID number is used for KYC verification only and is stored securely.

2

Use a referral code (optional)

If a friend referred you, enter their referral code on Step 1 of sign-up. This earns them a R10 bonus once you make your first payment, and helps grow the StokvelX community.

3

Log in

After registering you are logged in automatically and taken to your Dashboard. On future visits, go to the Login page, enter your email and password, and click Login.

4

Add a bank account

Go to Profile → Bank Details and enter your bank name and account number. This is required before you can withdraw money from your wallet to your bank account.

💡 Pro Tip Use a university email address (e.g. @uct.ac.za, @wits.ac.za) when signing up — it helps with KYC verification and builds trust within your circle.
🏠

3. Joining a Circle

▼

A savings circle (stokvel) is a group of people who each contribute a fixed amount every week. One member receives the full pot each week, rotating through all positions.

1

Browse available circles

Click Join a Circle in the sidebar. You'll see all open circles with details: weekly contribution amount, number of members, spots available, net payout, and duration.

2

Filter by type

Use the filter tabs to narrow circles by type: Standard (classic rotating), Premium (fast-payout with priority positions), or Investment (longer cycle, higher returns).

3

Click "Join Circle"

Once you've chosen a circle, click Join Circle. You'll be added at the next available position. A pending contribution is created immediately — pay it to confirm your spot.

4

Pay your first contribution

Go to My Circles and click the Pay Contribution button. Choose wallet, EFT, card, or Ozow as your payment method.

⚠️ Important You cannot join a circle that is already full (all positions taken). Check the "Spots Left" indicator before attempting to join.

4. Making Payments

▼

Contributions must be paid every week by the due date. You can see your due dates on the My Circles page and on your Dashboard.

1

Go to My Circles

Click My Circles in the sidebar. Each circle shows your current week, due date, and whether your contribution is Paid, Pending, or Late.

2

Click "Pay Contribution"

Click the green Pay Contribution button on the circle card. The payment modal will show the amount due for the current week.

3

Choose your payment method

Select one of four options: Wallet (instant, deducted from your balance), EFT (bank transfer, 1–2 days), Card (debit/credit), or Ozow (instant EFT).

4

Confirm payment

After confirming, your contribution status updates to Paid instantly. The transaction also appears in your wallet history.

💡 Wallet Payments are Fastest Paying from your StokvelX wallet updates your status immediately. Top up your wallet in advance so you can pay with one click every week.
⚠️ Late Payments If you miss a payment deadline, your status changes to Late. You can still pay after the deadline, but late contributions may affect your standing in future circles. Pay on time to maintain a good reputation.
💰

5. Your Wallet

▼

Your StokvelX wallet is your central money hub. It holds your balance, receives payouts and referral bonuses, and lets you pay contributions instantly.

Adding Money to Your Wallet

1

Click "Add Money" on the Wallet page

Navigate to Wallet in the sidebar. Click the Add Money button.

2

Enter the amount

Type the amount you want to add. Minimum top-up is R10, maximum is R50,000 per transaction.

3

Choose your method and confirm

Select EFT, Card, or Ozow. Confirm and the funds will reflect in your wallet balance.

Withdrawing Money

1

Add a bank account first

Go to Profile and fill in your bank name and account number. You cannot withdraw without a saved bank account.

2

Click "Withdraw" on the Wallet page

Enter the amount (minimum R50). Your funds will be sent to your saved bank account within 1–2 business days.

ℹ️ What goes into your wallet automatically? When you receive a circle payout, the money lands directly in your StokvelX wallet. Referral bonuses are also credited automatically. You can leave the money in your wallet to pay future contributions, or withdraw it to your bank.
🎉

6. Payouts

▼

A payout is when the full week's contributions from all circle members are given to one member. Every member gets one payout during the cycle.

→

How payout amounts are calculated

Net Payout = (Weekly Contribution − Admin Fee) × Max Members
Example: (R250 − R10) × 10 members = R2,400

→

Your payout position

Positions are assigned when you join. You can see your position on the My Circles page (e.g. "Position 4 of 10"). Position 4 means you receive the payout in week 4 of the cycle.

→

Payout statuses

Paid — You've received this payout already.

Upcoming — Your payout is the next one scheduled (within 1–2 weeks).

Pending — Scheduled for a future week, not yet imminent.

→

Where does payout money go?

Payout funds are credited directly to your StokvelX wallet. You'll see a "Payout received" entry in your transaction history. You can then withdraw to your bank account at any time.

💡 Stay Paid Up to Keep Your Payout Date Your payout date is guaranteed as long as all members stay up to date. Encourage your circle members to pay on time so the rotation runs smoothly.

7. Referrals & Bonuses

▼

Earn extra money by inviting your friends and classmates to join StokvelX. Every successful referral puts cash in your wallet.

1

Find your referral code

Go to Referrals in the sidebar. Your unique referral code is displayed at the top (e.g. SIPHO10).

2

Share your link

Click Copy Referral Link to get your personal sign-up link. Share it via WhatsApp, Instagram, email, or anywhere your classmates will see it.

3

Your friend signs up and joins a circle

Your friend registers using your code. Their status shows as Pending in your referrals list until they make their first contribution payment.

4

Earn R10 per paid referral

Once your friend makes their first ever contribution, you automatically earn R10 credited to your wallet. No action needed — it's instant.

🏆 Milestone Bonus — R50 for 10 Referrals! Once 10 of your referred friends have made their first contribution, you receive a one-off R50 milestone bonus on top of your regular referral earnings. That's R150 total for 10 referrals.

8. Circle Chat

▼

Each circle has its own group chat where members can communicate, confirm payments, and support each other.

→

Accessing chat

Click Circle Chat in the sidebar. Your circles appear in the left panel — click any circle name to switch to that chat.

→

Sending messages

Type your message in the input box at the bottom and press Enter or click the send button (➤). Messages are visible to all members of that circle only.

→

New messages load automatically

The chat polls for new messages every 5 seconds. You don't need to refresh the page to see new messages from your circle members.

⚠️ Chat Etiquette Keep conversations respectful and relevant to your circle. Reminders about payment deadlines and payout confirmations are always welcome. Avoid sharing personal banking details in the chat.

9. Profile & Settings

▼

Keep your profile up to date so your circle members and the platform can identify you correctly.

Updating Your Profile

1

Go to Profile

Click Profile in the sidebar. You'll see your current personal information and bank details.

2

Edit your details

You can update: First name, last name, phone number, university, and student number. Your email address cannot be changed after registration.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

▼
What happens if I can't pay my contribution on time? ▼
Your contribution status changes to Late. You can still pay after the deadline — the system will accept it. However, consistently late payments may affect your eligibility for future premium circles. Always communicate with your circle members via chat if you're having difficulties.
Can I be in more than one circle at the same time? ▼
Yes! You can join multiple circles simultaneously. Each circle has its own contribution schedule and payout date. Your Dashboard shows a combined view of all your circles and upcoming obligations.
How long does a wallet withdrawal take? ▼
Withdrawals are processed within 1–2 business days. The funds are sent to the bank account you've saved in your Profile. Make sure your account details are correct before withdrawing.
What is KYC verification and why do I need it? ▼
KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It's a regulatory requirement for financial platforms. Your ID number is used to verify your identity. Verified accounts have full access to all features including withdrawals. Unverified accounts may have limited access.
Can I leave a circle once I've joined? ▼
Leaving a circle mid-cycle is not recommended as it disrupts other members' payout schedule. If you have an urgent need to leave, contact support. If you have already received your payout, you're expected to continue contributing until the cycle completes.
Why is my referral showing as "Pending"? ▼
A referral stays Pending until your friend makes their first contribution payment in a circle. Simply signing up is not enough — they need to actually join a circle and pay their first weekly contribution. Once they do, your bonus of R10 is credited automatically.
Is my money safe on StokvelX? ▼
All financial transactions are recorded and logged in full. Your wallet balance is stored securely in the database and every debit and credit is tracked. Payout amounts are calculated mathematically based on actual contributions received — there is no guesswork.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▼
Currently, password resets are handled by contacting support at support@stokvelx.co.za. If you're already logged in, go to Profile → Change Password to update your password at any time.
What is the admin fee and who gets it? ▼
The admin fee (typically R10 per contribution) is deducted from each member's weekly contribution before the payout pot is calculated. It goes to StokvelX to cover platform operating costs, development, and support. The net payout you receive is after this fee has been deducted.
Can I pay for multiple weeks at once? ▼
Currently the system processes one contribution per week per circle. You pay the current week's contribution and next week's will become due after the week advances. This keeps the circle rotation fair and transparent for all members.
